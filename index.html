Areas of work

Work at height

A substantial proportion of our work is carried out above head height: double-volume rooms, stairwells, apex gable walls and exposed roof trusses.

We use mobile scaffold towers and extension ladders in these areas, as our project photography records.

Mobile access equipment — we provide our own scaffold towers and extension ladders
Ceilings and beams — we cut beams in by brush and roller the adjoining surfaces
Exposed timber — we cut trusses in by hand where they adjoin painted surfaces
Floor protection — we sheet floors for the duration of the works
