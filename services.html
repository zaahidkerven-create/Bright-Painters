Services

Ten services, one discipline

The services below are those we have registered. Each is set out with its usual scope of works.

Walls & ceilings

Decoration to internal and external wall and ceiling surfaces.

Interior painting

Internal walls, ceilings, cornices, skirtings and architraves, with floors and fittings covered before work begins.

Exterior painting

External walls, fascias, barge boards and rainwater goods. Loose material is removed and cracks made good before coating.

Feature walls

Contrasting colours applied to individual wall surfaces, with coverage assessed in daylight and further coats applied where required.

Surface preparation

Filling, sanding and priming of wall surfaces, including making good to chased channels and damaged plaster, prior to decoration.

Work at height

Surfaces above head height, worked from our own access equipment.

Roof painting

Coatings to concrete tile and profiled steel roof coverings, including ridges and flashings.

Double-volume ceilings

Ceilings and upper wall surfaces in double-volume rooms and stairwells, decorated from a mobile scaffold tower.

Exposed trusses

Board surfaces sealed and the adjoining timber cut in by hand where trusses are left exposed.

Apex walls & gables

Upper wall surfaces cut in by brush, with the remainder roller applied.

Timber finishes

Opaque, stained and clear finishes to interior and exterior timber.

Wood painting

Opaque finishes to joinery, with knotting, priming and undercoating carried out before the finishing coat.

Wood staining

Penetrating stains worked into the grain and finished to an even tone, interior or exterior.

Wood varnishing

Clear varnish applied in successive coats and denibbed between applications, in the appropriate grade.

Deck painting

Timber decking prepared by removing existing finishes and sanding, before sealing or coating.

Metalwork, doors & furniture

Preparation and finishing of joinery, steelwork and loose furniture.

Gates & burglar bars

Gates, burglar bars and security screens prepared and coated, with rust taken back to bright metal and primed.

Door painting

Internal and external doors and frames, including the edges, with ironmongery removed before coating.

Furniture painting

Furniture prepared and finished, sprayed or brushed, on site or away from the property.

Garage doors & railings

Garage doors, balustrades and railings prepared and finished in an enamel coating system.

Process

How a project is carried out

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    Contact us by telephone or WhatsApp with a description of the work required and the property address.

  2. 02

    Site assessment

    We attend the property to assess the surfaces and take measurements.

  3. 03

    Quotation

    We issue a quotation setting out the works, materials and labour.

  4. 04

    Works & handover

    We carry out the works in sequence, and inspect the completed work with you on conclusion.
